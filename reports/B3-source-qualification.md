## Primary method and evidence boundaries

[Kettell, McInnis and Zhao, April 2022 working paper](https://business.columbia.edu/sites/default/files-efs/imce-uploads/CEASA/Events%20Page/PEAD_Declined_over_time.pdf), abstract, introduction and section 3.1.2, was read directly. Its main surprise measure uses same-quarter prior-year accounting-income change scaled by prior-year market equity, rather than analyst consensus. Its main return window begins two trading dates after the announcement and extends through the next quarterly announcement. The authors report attenuation associated with weaker earnings-news persistence; they also distinguish early and later drift windows. These claims do not establish our modern five-session, long-only adaptation or a locally executable net benefit. Next-quarter persistence classifications are explanatory research variables, not a known-at-announcement trading filter.

Martineau's [author publication page](https://www.charlesmartineau.com/) was read and confirms the related 2022 publication. This increment's attempted CFR PDF endpoints and publisher article returned inaccessible/403 responses; its full methodology was not newly verified. Earlier imported decay claims and [prior event-continuation source reading](../docs/sources/EVENT_CONTINUATION.md) retain their own access grades. No abstract or imported verdict becomes a local rejection. Original dossiers remain unchanged.

Accounting earnings change, analyst forecast error, standardized unexpected earnings and price reaction are distinct measures. Their normalization, input populations, clocks and holding windows must be registered separately. This increment does not adopt canonical SUE thresholds, next-quarter labels or the inherited proposal's costs/support thresholds as an executed experiment.

## Ingredient and checks

[Earnings difference contract](../fixtures/earnings_surprise.py) pairs an explicitly qualified first-print actual with the latest complete-ledger consensus vintage available **strictly before** release. Exact security, fiscal period, EPS metric, currency, share basis, accounting basis and units must match. Actual release/publication must agree and actual arrival must precede the decision. Unknown authority, restated actual, late arrivals, ambiguous versions and mismatched bases block selection. No pre-release consensus produces an unknown result, rather than an inferred beat.

It reuses the [as-of version selector](../fixtures/fundamental_vintages.py). Integer clocks, first-print labels and authority fields are synthetic assertions; real source semantics and completeness still need evidence. Output is a raw currency-per-share difference, neither a percentage surprise nor SUE. Zero/negative consensus values remain legitimate inputs to subtraction; division by them is not introduced.

[64 independent hand-derived checks](../scripts/check_earnings_surprise.py) include consensus 1 available before release, a later revised consensus 2, and actual 1.25: raw difference is 0.25. A revision or estimate arriving exactly at release cannot replace that consensus. Checks cover later decisions, order invariance, negative/zero values, ambiguous vintages, first-print, clock and basis guards. They establish synthetic correctness, not actual feed qualification.
