# EQ-001-P1 — First stock pilot: inconclusive

Completed October 5, 2026. This is an exploratory daily-bar price-only proxy on a fixed convenience sample of 20 established US stocks, not quoted fills, a funded portfolio, a representative large/mid-cap universe or fresh holdout evidence.

Fixed rule: close above prior daily SMA50 above prior SMA100; candidate also negative trailing two-session close change. Control buys the unrestricted uptrend. Prior price/liquidity/availability gates applied. Next-session Open entry and fifth holding-session Close exit; assumed 10 bps roundtrip costs, 20 bps stress. No parameter grid. Signal windows September–December 2025 and January–June 2026 with crossing outcomes purged; both remain exposed development evidence.

| Later-period metric | Uptrend control | Pullback subset |
|---|---:|---:|
| Scored observations | 629 | 241 |
| +2% touch within three sessions | 50.2% | 55.6% |
| +3% touch within three sessions | 33.5% | 37.8% |
| Mean five-session proxy return after 10 bps | −0.325% | −0.120% |
| Mean after 20 bps stress | −0.425% | −0.220% |

Date-matched mean improvement was +0.199 percentage points, descriptive dependence-aware 95% interval −0.581 to +0.993 pp. Quarter signs differed. Decision: **inconclusive**. A target touch can coexist with a negative terminal return and does not establish an executable target sale or call profit.

All variants, failures and corrections are retained privately. Final 13 deterministic assertions and 13 independently coded ledger reconciliations passed. These validate the proxy against extracted inputs; they do not independently certify original feeds. Two adapter failures occurred before scored outcomes and were corrected without changing thresholds.

Material stock-specific missingness, source-close variation, convenience-universe bias, overlapping signals, assumed costs and absent dividend cash qualify conclusions. Eighty-three end-of-window observations were censored, not assigned wins/losses. Daily bars cannot resolve within-bar touch ordering. Raw input data, detailed ledgers, private logs and internal paths are not published here.

Next: trace missing-reference and close-source provenance before any separately versioned broader comparison. No winning ticker selection, target-grid tuning, strategy promotion or calls justified by this pilot.
