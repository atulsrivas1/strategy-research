# E4 — Fundamental-vintage prerequisite qualification

October 8, 2026. Related story: [SR-047](https://github.com/atulsrivas1/strategy-research/issues/66), release [M7](../docs/releases/M7_PLAN.md), accepted [shared design](../docs/releases/M6_RECEIPT.md). Scientific status: untested. This bounded increment implements synthetic as-of fundamental version selection. It does not implement a growth-breakout scanner or qualify historical fundamentals.

The author-hosted [Novy-Marx working paper](https://mysimon.rochester.edu/novy-marx/research/FMFM.pdf), abstract and section 2.1 (printed page 4), was read directly. Its standardized earnings surprise uses year-over-year EPS changes scaled by prior earnings innovations; its separate announcement-return measure uses a three-day market-adjusted window. These are distinct ingredients from an O'Neil-style percentage-growth screen. The paper does not verify canonical CANSLIM, VCP or Darvas rules or our proposed five-session adaptation. No source performance was locally reproduced.

The [IBD CANSLIM entry URL](https://www.investors.com/ibd-university/can-slim/) redirected to a truncated beginner article, without complete canonical rules. The [Minervini site](https://minervini.com/) was inaccessible in this access attempt. Primary books were not read in this increment. Preserve original imported dossiers and their access grades; secondary thresholds remain unverified. The causal contraction scanner and its exact rules remain to be designed and independently checked before replay.

[Version selector](../fixtures/fundamental_vintages.py) requires an explicit security, fiscal period and metric, a complete supplied version ledger, qualified source and historical availability, unique version IDs and finite values. It selects the latest version available by the decision. Period end alone cannot establish publication time. Unknown provenance, malformed versions and ambiguous latest timestamps block selection. Future restatements cannot change an earlier selected value. Negative or zero EPS values remain valid observations; this selector does not pretend that percentage growth from such a denominator is defined.

[Hand-derived checks](../scripts/check_fundamental_vintages.py) cover original EPS 1 available at toy clock 9 versus revised EPS 2 available at 13: decision 10 selects 1, decision 13 selects 2. They also cover permutations, empty eligibility, duplicates, conflicting versions, clock boundaries, missing metadata and nonfinite values. Integer clocks and source-authority labels are synthetic assertions, not certified historical feeds. No historical fundamental values were read.

Empirical blockers: primary canonical rule access; decision-time complete fundamental vintages and announcement clocks; causal contraction/high/volume definitions; qualified action-adjusted prices, calendar and point-in-time universe; relative-strength cohort; stock/market context clocks; execution/accounting; preregistered controls, costs, uncertainty and splits. Existing [D1](D1-session-feasibility.md) and [D2](D2-source-qualification.md) prerequisites remain incomplete. Keep a frozen scanner-only matched baseline and preregister context before any outcomes. Historical trials remain zero and final access disabled. Full SR-047 stays open at partial Test; M7 remains planned, unreleased. Agent evidence inspection uses the owner review waiver; no independent approval claimed.
