Scientific status: untested locally. This increment builds synthetic indicator machinery; no historical price, indicator, scanner, outcome or lake data was read.

[StockCharts provider documentation](https://chartschool.stockcharts.com/table-of-contents/technical-indicators-and-overlays/technical-indicators/relative-strength-index-rsi), calculation and smoothing notes, was read directly. It specifies simple-average initialization followed by Wilder recursive smoothing and explains dependence on the history length. Its implementation uses substantial pre-chart history. Our declared seed is explicit; no StockCharts parity or converged warmup is claimed. Wilder's original book was not read. Fully flat initialization leaves both averages zero; our local convention returns undefined rather than arbitrarily adopting 0, 50 or 100.

[Connors and Dorkins' Improved R2 article](https://tradingmarkets.com/recent/the_improved_r2_strategy_84_correct_with_just_6_rules_-674361), rules and scope, was read directly from its originating publisher. Its index-oriented variant uses a trend filter and consecutive declining RSI values, with a threshold exit. It differs from a single oversold-threshold individual-stock scanner with a fixed five-session hold. Its same-close entry also requires separate causal execution qualification. No reported article performance was locally reproduced. Original book thresholds/sample and related stock variants remain unverified; primary access to one variant does not qualify all imported proposals.

The attempted archived Nagel liquidity-supply PDF was inaccessible in this attempt. No SPY experiment is proposed or rerun. Original dossiers and earlier research receipts remain frozen.

[Seeded RSI(2) ingredient](../fixtures/rsi2.py) requires a declared immutable history start, exact ordered complete session ledger, one security/source/split basis, qualified prices/actions/calendar/initialization and available-before-decision clocks. Two close changes initialize gain/loss averages; subsequent changes use period-two Wilder recursion. Missing or future rows, reordered sessions, mixed provenance, invalid prices and unknown clocks block calculation. Fixed local Decimal precision isolates arithmetic from caller precision. Authority fields and integer clocks are synthetic declarations, not empirical source certification.

[53 independent rational and causal checks](../scripts/check_rsi2.py) include closes 100, 102, 101, 100, 101: RSI values are 200/3, 40, 200/3. Exact reduced-fraction oracles verify arithmetic; checks cover prefixes, rising/falling/flat cases, caller context, initialization/session gaps, identity/basis, availability and numeric failures. No scanner threshold or warmup variant was selected from outcomes.

Empirical blockers remain qualified original close/session/auction and action history, observed historical arrivals, point-in-time universe, exact frozen scanner/context/entry/exit, costs and execution/accounting. [D1 source limitations](D1-session-feasibility.md) apply; stored UTC-day data cannot silently become a complete regular-session close.
