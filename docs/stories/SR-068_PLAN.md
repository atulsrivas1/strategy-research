## Hypothesis and information value

A common research contract and complete applicability matrix can prevent inconsistent source interpretation, incompatible adaptations, causal mistakes, hidden trial search and false completion across the imported libraries. Expected benefit is auditable and comparable research decisions, not a trading-return promise. This is methodology design, not an experiment claiming a market edge.

## Baseline and experiment

One design cycle, zero market replays, model fits, threshold searches or final evaluations. Compare the proposed shared contract against existing per-story plans and lessons; use synthetic examples to expose omissions without outcome-driven rule selection.

Design deliverables must specify:

- Complete 76-dossier/40-family traceability and a requirements/applicability matrix: mechanism, canonical instrument/horizon/direction, allowed stock adaptation or defer disposition, source access grade, blockers and exact next qualification.
- A reusable intake/specification template: falsifiable question, source support/counterevidence, expected benefit, reason to retry, stock universe/action/adjustment conventions, field units/lineage and event/receipt/first-seen/available-at clocks, producer/access ownership and missing/stale/unknown/censored policies.
- Matched scanner-only control and one preregistered mechanism-specific stock/market context policy; aligned/conflicting/neutral/unknown definitions, immutable opportunities/weights, vetoed allocation cash, avoided losses and sacrificed winners net of costs. Do not inherit the rejected joint M2 gate without a new justified mechanism/version/budget.
- Chronological exposure/split/purge/embargo rules, protected final access, preregistration/trial registry and bounded search budgets; dependent-test/multiple-comparison accounting and complete failure/variant records. Previously seen periods stay development; final disabled.
- Evaluation requirements for fees/spreads/slippage/actions/dividends, turnover/exposure/overlap/cash/funding and feasible order timing. Explicitly separate modeled price proxies, observed fills and funded executable accounting; decide numeric criteria before dependent outcomes, not from known winners.
- Primary net metrics, support/precision requirements, dependence-aware paired uncertainty, concentration/robustness/cost stress and scoped rejected/inconclusive/promising/invalid verdicts. Eligibility for genuinely unused independent evaluation is a separate gate, not assumed by design completion.
- Reuse/parity and independent relevant correctness checks; result/receipt templates, privacy allowlist, exact source/artifact verification, eight-stage lifecycle and story/release acceptance. Agent inspection under the waiver is not independent approval.
- A dependency-aware execution sequence and stop/defer/resume rules, one active implementation and explicit later trial registration. Calls remain conditional; M4 excluded, M5's frozen predecessors unchanged; no automatic new session or schedule.
