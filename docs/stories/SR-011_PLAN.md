# SR-011 — Assess medium-horizon reversal and news-data feasibility

Parent: [E02](https://github.com/atulsrivas1/strategy-research/issues/9). Release plan: [M1](../releases/M1_PLAN.md). Backlog mapping: B-008. Current lifecycle is in the [Project](https://github.com/users/atulsrivas1/projects/4); readiness classification: **exploratory**.

## Hypothesis and information value

Industry and publication-time news controls may distinguish transitory reversal from fundamental repricing.

Expected information value: Medium/high, distinct mechanism but costly prerequisites. Effort: Medium feasibility; high eventual study; estimates describe planning complexity, not deadlines or velocity.
