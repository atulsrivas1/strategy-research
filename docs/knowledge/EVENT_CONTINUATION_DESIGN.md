# Proposed next stock hypothesis: earnings-event continuation

State: exploratory design/readiness note; empirical blocked and unrun. This proposes a different information mechanism after the two context overlays failed; no threshold, market source or winning historical bucket was tuned. Existing M2 budget is exhausted and the new idea is not an assigned M3 release.

Hypothesis: a genuinely positive earnings surprise signals fundamental repricing, with positive stock reaction exceeding the contemporaneous cash-market reaction potentially identifying five-session continuation. Expected benefit and falsification are explicit: efficient immediate pricing, common small beats, delayed entry, costs, revised consensus or insufficient event support may eliminate utility. [Sources and counterevidence](../sources/EVENT_CONTINUATION.md) do not establish a modern five-day edge.

Proposed scanner: actual EPS exceeds consensus demonstrably fixed before release, matched fiscal quarter/currency/share/accounting basis; qualified historical identity and price/liquidity rules. One event/security/quarter, equal weights across same-date eligible events. Never infer surprise from a price move or use a revised forecast.

Context proposal: at the close of the first full regular session after exact release, stock reaction from the last pre-release close must be positive and exceed the cash-index reaction over the identical interval. Record stock and market-relative aligned/conflicting/neutral/unknown states plus the absolute market sign. Vetoed original weights remain cash. The index is a context reference, never traded; relative return is not beta-neutral alpha. This does not reuse the rejected absolute-market-SMA gate.

Baseline is the unchanged positive-surprise scanner; one joint reaction/outperformance overlay, five-session next-open/fifth-close horizon. Same-date equal liquid-cohort exposure is a secondary diagnostic of scanner economics. Proposed primary5bps/side; matching10/20bps and candidate15vsbaseline10bps stresses. Proposed acceptance requires positive net scanner and context increment>10bps, lower95% calendar-block interval>0, positive development/cost stresses/subperiods/concentration; nonpositive increments reject context only. Exact supported chronology, accounting and numerical protocol must be qualified/frozen before any run. No Friday, SUE-size, volume, stop/target, horizon or ticker search.

Proposed support safeguard:100distinct usable events,50event decision dates and10names per period, not a power guarantee. The inherited18-name short chronology may be inadequate; do not enlarge universe/horizon to manufacture significance. Existing exposed periods stay development, final disabled. One future comparison would need a separately explicit bounded budget; none runs here.
