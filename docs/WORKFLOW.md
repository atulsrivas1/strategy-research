# Evidence-gated research workflow

Scope: US liquid large/mid-cap individual stocks, long shares, 2–10 trading sessions; bought calls conditional on stock evidence. ETFs excluded and intraday/monthly extensions deferred or feasibility-only pending explicit scope decisions. Downside analysis supports suitable long entries; no short selling or puts.

Read [brief](RESEARCH_BRIEF.md), [knowledge](PROJECT_KNOWLEDGE.md), [index](RESEARCH_INDEX.md), [lessons](LEARNINGS.md), [backlog](BACKLOG.md), [handoff](SESSION_HANDOFF.md), selected plan and live Project before choosing work. Adopt equity-features' [release-based delivery](DELIVERY_POLICY.md) and [roadmap](ROADMAP.md).

Before outcome access freeze mechanism/hypothesis, baseline, formulas/units/clocks, availability/eligibility, dataset versions, chronological splits and purge, costs, metrics, trial budget and quantitative decision thresholds. Previously inspected data remains development across projects. Protect the final holdout; repeated evaluation consumes independence. Missing fields block the affected execution/claim.

Use small independent hand-derived fixtures before market replay. Separate adapters, calculation functions, signals, labels and accounting; source features and an exact accepted library version can be reused only after qualification/parity. This research project does not take ownership of library jobs or source production.

Preserve all runs/variants/failures and original provenance. A bug invalidates evidence; a negative result rejects tested scope only. Report net economics, credible execution stress, dependence-aware uncertainty and concentration, not win rate alone. Distinguish source claims, proxy touches, assumed-price returns, executable fills and funded accounting. Literature and imported reports are not local replication.

Each story delivers complete relevant source/method/report documentation, commands/manifests privately, scoped interpretation, lessons and next question. Publish sanitized aggregates/methodology and synthetic/expressly licensed fixtures only. No private data, detailed proprietary ledgers, raw chats, secrets or internal paths. Live trading, paid purchases and brokerage changes require separate direction.
