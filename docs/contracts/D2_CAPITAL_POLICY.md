## Findings from the delivered source

The current v3 tranche is initial capital divided by D*(1+maximum fee), D=250. At most five ordinary decision cohorts are held concurrently. Flat prices and zero fees therefore imply gross exposure5/250=2%, independent of stock selection. Equal-cohort and scanner receive the same initial tranche and timing; their low deployment is already matched. The policy bounds all lifetime purchase costs by initial cash, which protects funding but is not a reusable fully deployed portfolio. Scaling reported returns by inverse average exposure would change the model without reproducing funding, compounding, risk or costs.

The existing ledger moves net sale proceeds directly into its modeled aggregate cash. That is its disclosed open-fill research convention, not evidence of broker settlement availability. Recycling needs a separate spendable cash/receivable boundary. Most covered US securities transactions use T+1 business-day settlement from May28,2024; cash-account funding depends on settlement circumstances. The SEC materials do not certify our market-date calendar or exact broker buying-power time. Read [T+1 bulletin](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/new-t1-settlement-cycle-what-investors-need-know-investor-bulletin) and [cash-account bulletin](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-bulletins/updated-9). Our spendable-only convention below is a conservative research choice, not a statement that every use of unsettled proceeds is prohibited.

## Selected capital policy

Use seven independent sleeves, initially capital/7 each, with no transfer or borrowing. For decision ordinal j assign sleeve j modulo7. Keep the existing five-session hold and +2-session entry. An ordinary exit at entry+5 creates a net receivable; model its eligible transfer at the close of the next admitted settlement business day and permit spending at the following admitted market open. Seven comes from five held sessions plus one ordinary settlement session plus the next-open convention, not optimization against returns. It is not a promise of 100% exposure: in flat fee-free uninterrupted ordinary sessions the illustrative steady gross share is5/7. Holiday timing, fees, gains/losses, missing entries and end effects change actual exposure.

Freeze a dated entry/exit/settlement/eligibility map before outcome reads. Distinguish market sessions from settlement business days and record source/version/clock assumption for both. If certified metadata is unavailable, a clearly labeled calendar proxy may support exploratory execution under the evidence policy; do not claim actual broker funding or assume a market holiday list proves settlement eligibility. Freeze the proxy before outcomes. Longer known delays prevent reuse until eligible; no same-day sale rescue or automatic extra sleeve. Unknown eligibility remains unresolved. Record every scheduled omission without pruning decision dates; incomplete primary schedules cannot support a qualified primary verdict.

At the close before entry, freeze total purchase notional B=that sleeve's spendable cash/(1+0.003). This close is after the decision and before the modeled fill, so this is preregistered causal entry sizing, not an entry veto or hindsight revision. Buy the same top3 equally at the entry open, or the same18 benchmark constituents equally. Deduct actual fee from spendable cash; retain unused maximum-fee reserve in the sleeve. Fractional shares, exact stored-price values and causal split adjustments carry forward. Never use a future exit, tomorrow's mark or another sleeve's NAV in B. If an old holding is censored/unresolved, its sleeve cannot recycle. Zero available cash cannot create a zero-notional successful entry.

Daily NAV = spendable cash + known net receivables + marked open positions, summed across sleeves. A sale removes the position and adds a receivable once. A maturity transfers receivable to spendable cash without changing NAV. Cash interest and borrowing remain zero. Missing marks or known unresolved actions produce unknown affected NAV; income/dividends/other actions are not fabricated. Final known receivables remain part of NAV even if settlement follows the last price date; terminal cash realization is reported separately.

## Benchmark and context controls

Primary scanner and equal18-cohort benchmark each use their own seven sleeves and the identical policy, initial capital, scheduled entries/exits, settlement map, costs and cash return. The benchmark is generated independently of scanner ranking success. Each arm reinvests its own realized wealth; later dollar notionals and exposure may therefore differ. This tests the funded wealth paths of two policies, not equal dollar risk at every instant. Report both exposure paths and funding failures; do not normalize one arm to the other's realized winners or silently call them volatility matched.

The fixed five-session conflicting-stock veto remains a diagnostic control only. Its intended retained orders use the scanner's pre-entry frozen notionals, no reweighting, and vetoed fractions remain cash. Reference sizing is a controlled research comparison, not a standalone broker strategy. Candidate cash is accounted independently. If candidate funding cannot cover a retained batch, record the full batch as unfilled/funding mismatch; the isolated overlay verdict becomes inconclusive and skipped winners/losses are not attributed to the veto. Candidate mismatch does not contaminate an otherwise complete scanner/benchmark primary comparison. Implement primary and diagnostic usability flags separately. Do not force funds, shrink retained positions, or couple the primary scanner budget to the candidate's balance. Stock aligned/conflicting/neutral/unknown mapping and market unknown/admit convention stay frozen; no new context efficacy test is proposed.
