## Question and adaptation

Primary: does a fixed top-three long-only cross-sectional scanner beat a same-date equal-cohort benchmark net of modeled fees over a broader development window? [Kenneth French's daily momentum construction](https://mba.tuck.dartmouth.edu/pages/faculty/ken.french/Data_Library/det_mom_factor_daily.html) actually read for this design uses months of prior-return formation. Our252-minus21 modeled-session close ratio borrows longer formation and a skipped recent period. It differs explicitly from the old20-session rule; five-session holding, long-only top-three, convenience universe, equal weights and unverified price adjustments mean it is an adaptation, not replication of that factor. Formation and holding horizon are separate choices.

Freeze18 previous convenience symbols: AMZN, GOOGL, META, NVDA, JPM, BAC, XOM, CVX, JNJ, PFE, PG, KO, HD, WMT, CAT, UNH, DIS, CSCO. Historical membership, survivors and adjusted-return authority remain unknown, so results can only be conditional exploratory evidence. Require an explicit positive stable-ID/symbol map; never replace missing members or merge ticker histories.

For each decision i, rank exact fractions close(i−21)/close(i−252)−1. Require all253 history rows for every member, with known arrival times no later than modeled UTC day end. Unknown arrival clocks stay disclosed proxies. Boundary ties or missing/late members produce unresolved scanner dates. The benchmark is still planned independently on every original date. Enter at modeled open i+2; exit open i+7. No stops, target changes or grid.

The old five-session stock-consistency veto is retained solely as a fixed diagnostic negative control: conflicting if current close is below close(i−5), aligned above, neutral equal; unknown market state always recorded. Keep aligned/neutral/unknown, veto conflicting, retain original notionals/cash. Record avoided net losses and sacrificed winners; **no filter-promotion criterion**, tuning or reversal of its old rejection. One primary scanner-versus-benchmark question plus this declared secondary diagnostic, not hidden multiple primary tests.

## Fixed funding and missing observations

Initial research capital1, fractional shares, zero borrowing/interest. For D planned decisions reserve1/D cash per decision; purchase notional is1/(D×1.003). Split among3 scanner positions or18 benchmark positions, preserve every original size in the veto arm. Sum of all purchases plus maximum stress fees is at most initial cash even if all earlier lots lose all value. No proceeds-dependent resizing or dynamic affordability selection. This deliberately conservative capital schedule has low simultaneous utilization; do not present it as a fully invested live mandate or annualize its returns as such.

Use exact rational quantities/cash/fees/NAV, modeled same-open exits before entries, then close marks. Unfilled entry batches, censored missing exits and unknown NAV are retained. Require consistent price units; currency conversion is not implemented. No observed fills, integer-lot sizing, interest, spreads/impact, fixed broker commissions, dividend/split transformation or market-regime filter is claimed. Unknown source/action/session evidence is nonblocking uncertainty for exploratory use, while actual numerical/unit errors must be corrected.
