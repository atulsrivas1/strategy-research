## Enforced preregistration binding

The v3 development preflight checks the immutable registration hash, exact protocol, full executed source coverage and normalized-LF source hashes, fixed trial-registry/input identity, and complete admitted chronology/protection metadata before claiming budget or reading the bundle. The guard snapshots the registration and the loader rechecks it. Line-ending normalization is explicit; original exact-byte snapshots are retained. This prevents a stale or redirected registration from silently authorizing altered source, parameters, inputs or protected-date metadata. The same correction allowance remains registered/unspent; preliminary registration is retained as provenance, not a second trial.
