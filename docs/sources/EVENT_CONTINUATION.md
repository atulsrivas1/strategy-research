# Event continuation: targeted primary reading, not local replication

Accessed October7,2026. DellaVigna and Pollet, [Investor Inattention and Friday Earnings Announcements](https://eml.berkeley.edu/~sdellavi/wp/earnfr080204.pdf), author-hosted February2008 version: abstract/introduction, data construction and drift-horizon discussion read. Underreaction supplies a mechanism, but the reported Friday differential largely develops beyond the first ten sessions; it does not establish today's long-only five-day edge.

Christensen, Timmermann and Veliyev, [Warp Speed Price Moves: Jumps after Earnings Announcements](https://arxiv.org/html/2601.08962v2), January2026 arXiv version of November2024 manuscript: abstract/introduction, sections6.1–6.2 and conclusion read. Reported rapid after-hours repricing and spread/latency sensitivity in recent subsamples challenge simple exploitation. Their short-horizon after-hours strategy differs from the proposed daily continuation. Full technical proofs not reviewed; no paper replication or ETF method adopted.

Local inference: prioritize event-time/consensus-vintage feasibility, preserve contradictory evidence and keep the five-session strategy untested. No literature return is treated as a local trading result.
