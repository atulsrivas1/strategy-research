## Build order and stop rules

1. Build outcome-free manifest/as-of/calendar/feature admission adapters first, after exact source contracts qualify. Reuse accepted package capabilities; qualify new capabilities independently.
2. Extend original-weight paired accounting, guarded label access, exposure/trial registry, evaluator and evidence packaging. Separate research artifacts from source producers; no duplicate upstream jobs.
3. Implement one qualified M7 scanner/context pair only after accepted design and its specific preregistration. Provisional D1, D2, E4 ordering stays until source qualification justifies change.
4. Event/fundamental/insider tooling follows source-clock/vintage feasibility. A missing pre-event vintage cannot be rebuilt from current revisions; record acquisition/capture/blocker instead.
5. Order-level/auction/borrow/options/non-equity needs remain designed but deferred/conditional. Their contracts do not activate scope.

If source authority, historical clock or required observations are unknown, stop empirical admission. Preserve blocked feasibility as evidence; do not invent data, substitute an undisclosed proxy or silently reduce scope. A prospective recorder only adds future observations; it cannot rehabilitate previously exposed history.
