# Shared strategy-research design v1 — October 8, 2026

This is SR-068's common design, covering 76 archived dossiers, 40 existing family stories and all required capabilities, including missing ones. It defines qualification and build paths; it does not claim a strategy edge or admit every existing dataset. [Complete applicability matrix](APPLICABILITY.md), [machine-readable source mapping](APPLICABILITY.json), [24 requirement contracts](REQUIREMENTS.json), [build register](BUILD_REGISTER.md), [templates](TEMPLATES.md) and [independent toy walkthroughs](WALKTHROUGHS.md) form one design package.

## 1. Objective, scope and decisions

Research US individual stocks, long shares, with 2–10-session horizons. Five-session rules are proposed adaptations; source futures/monthly/long-short evidence is not replication of them. Assess reproducible net economic benefit with uncertainty against a frozen matched scanner baseline. Source qualification, scientific verdict and delivery completion are separate decisions. Missing data blocks admission rather than disproving a mechanism. Negative/inconclusive findings may complete faithfully delivered experiments; bugs invalidate affected evidence.

M0/M1 accepted restricted science remains immutable. M1 is inconclusive/unpromoted. M2's two modeled context comparisons rejected their declared benefit criteria and exhausted 2/2 budget; strict observed execution/funding, historical arrival, applicable full parity and genuinely unused independent evidence remain open. Do not retune the rejected gate against known losers. Common design does not reopen a trial or complete those gates. M3 bought calls need supported stock evidence and options qualification; M4 excluded/deferred, even though its needs are designed. M5 frozen equity-features R5–R13/applicable M2/M3 predecessor list remains unchanged.

## 2. Architecture and evidence boundaries

Flow: immutable source/version → qualified as-of inputs → fixed scanner opportunities/weights → decision-time stock/market context → matched control/candidate execution → funded or explicitly proxy ledger → guarded labels → paired evaluator → scoped evidence receipt. Each edge records exact IDs, input/output hashes, units, clocks, policy/config/code versions and approval/admission state. No market-outcome access in this design's fixtures.

Keep three physical/logical zones: source-preserving metadata/input adapters; preregistered decisions and exposure/trial registry; separate outcomes/evaluation. Input adapters cannot import outcome modules or join post-event return columns. A future guarded loader checks frozen protocol, input authority, dependency receipts, exposed-period labels and remaining budget BEFORE label I/O. Research copies remain separate from another owner's producer/runtime jobs. Never union curated/prepared overlaps blindly; choose one immutable canonical source per observation and retain aliases/duplicate lineage.

Suggested research-side interfaces (requirements, not deployed tooling): `qualify_inputs(manifest, contract)` emits admission/blockers; `scan(asof_inputs, scanner_spec)` emits immutable opportunities; `context(asof_inputs, overlay_spec)` emits separate stock/market states and action; `simulate(opportunities, execution_spec)` emits ledger and censor status; `evaluate(paired_ledger, evaluation_spec)` emits aggregate evidence and uncertainty. Reject schema/clock/version mismatch. Avoid building a universal trading engine before one dependency-qualified question needs it.

## 3. Data and as-of contracts

Every consumed field must specify security ID and symbol validity interval, source/provider/license/access owner, dataset/capability/version/hash, schema/type/unit, session/timezone/DST/calendar, event time, receipt/observation time, publication/first-seen time, derived available-at rule, upstream dependencies, revision/adjustment convention, freshness and missingness. Never replace a missing receipt by partition/effective/report-end time. A reconstructed available-at rule is modeled and must stay labeled; it cannot qualify observed historical arrival.

Admission requires source version and applicable quality/causal checks plus `available_at <= decision_time`; exact equality is permitted only when the registered decision/order convention makes it feasible. Expired freshness, bad timestamp flags, stale references, late revisions, missing eligible identity and incomplete sessions remain explicit states. Use unknown context rather than filling from future data. Unknown handling (retain or veto) must be frozen per mechanism and tested independently; absence of evidence is not automatically bearish or harmless.

Daily bars and technical windows require exact completed-session semantics, warmup/missing-bar rule, split/dividend/raw-versus-adjusted price treatment, symbol transitions and independent source parity. Restrict a universe honestly if point-in-time membership is unavailable; the 18-name restricted cohort does not certify broad large/mid-cap eligibility or market breadth. A normalizer can be built from qualified inputs; missing historical receipt, quote sequence or consensus vintage cannot be reconstructed reliably from final snapshots.

Current private inventory establishes selected metadata/schema existence and reads producer PASS receipts. Import fidelity, source validity, mathematical validity, historical availability and execution qualification are distinct evidence classes. Watchlists/references exist with producer verification; remaining source clocks and full baseline acceptance must be checked independently at consumption. Detailed source locations/ledgers remain private. Do not certify every file or currently running process from a stale handoff.

## 4. Scanner and context specification

Each experiment has exactly one versioned primary scanner and one preregistered context policy unless a larger search is explicitly budgeted. Preserve original opportunities, timestamps, intended weights and eligibility before outcomes. Keep scanner-only control and scanner-plus-context candidate under identical execution/cost/calendar/chronology assumptions. Primary units include all eligible opportunities, including unknown, vetoed, unfilled and entered-censored observations; do not publish resolved-only profit as total strategy profit.

Record stock and market alignment separately as aligned/conflicting/neutral/unknown, exact fields/thresholds/versions and causal reasons. A contrarian scanner may rationally act during weakness; do not require every feature to be positive. Any combined action/unknown policy is fixed before outcomes. Vetoed weight remains cash, no reranking/rescaling retained names. Report avoided losses AND sacrificed winners, their net values, participation, unknown coverage and turnover; gate efficacy is unproven until a qualified experiment supports it.

Provisional first M7 qualification is D1: proposed completed close strictly above the maximum high over the prior 20 completed sessions, excluding the signal session, with proposed next-session-open/five-session proxy. This is a proposed restricted stock adaptation, not a validated or fully frozen experiment. D2 needs a justified formation window and point-in-time cross-sectional ranks; canonical monthly long-short evidence stays distinct. E4 needs past-only completed-bar pattern availability and separately qualified fundamentals if consumed. No numeric market threshold or empirical budget is chosen from this design's toy examples.

## 5. Execution, costs and portfolio accounting

Declare one evidence level: modeled daily-price proxy; quote-supported historical executable model; or observed fill evidence. They are not interchangeable. Record exact entry/exit/session convention, order type/delay, quote bid/ask/age/size, gap/halt/unfilled policy, fees/spread/slippage/impact, capacity/liquidity, actions/dividends and missing/censored exits. No same-bar stop/target order inferred from OHLC alone. Trades/TBBO can provide trade-triggered BBO observations; they do not establish continuous quote persistence or queue-confirmed fills. [Databento TBBO](https://databento.com/docs/schemas-and-data-formats/tbbo) describes trade-triggered BBO; [MBP-1](https://databento.com/docs/schemas-and-data-formats/mbp-1) describes top-level book updates. Read October 8, 2026 for this distinction, not local feed certification.

Funded accounting needs owner capital/risk constraints before dependent simulation: capital, per-position/portfolio exposure/loss/drawdown/turnover bounds and feasible order model. Reserve cash, enforce integer quantities and concurrent positions, retain veto allocation in cash, account for actions/dividends/fees/unresolved obligations and reconcile all debits/credits independently. Simple weight-return toy arithmetic is not funded account validation. Unknown exits remain censored; no zero-return imputation disguised as observed P&L. Stress missing outcomes separately and label the stress hypothetical.

## 6. Chronology, search and evaluation

Register exact question/mechanism/falsifier, source support/counterevidence, immutable code/config/input/scanner/overlay/evaluator versions, eligible universe, chronological development/validation intervals, purge/embargo based on actual label/holding overlap, exposure history and finite search budget before dependent outcomes. Previously inspected windows remain development even across projects. Final dates null and access disabled until SR-020 eligibility and genuinely unused/prospective evidence qualify. Final access guard precedes loader I/O; no directory naming creates an untouched holdout.

Choose primary net paired economic metric, opportunity/date/sleeve aggregation unit and minimum economic improvement, minimum effective sample/support, maximum uncertainty/acceptable concentration, resampling unit/block method/seed/repetitions, cost/funding stress and exact decision thresholds before outcomes. These values are REQUIRED experiment fields, intentionally unset here; blank values block empirical admission. No universal numeric floor inferred from synthetic returns. Date/holding dependence, overlapping opportunities and repeated candidate searches must be reflected in uncertainty and disclosed trial counts. If appropriate multiplicity adjustment cannot be justified, report that limit and avoid a confirmation claim.

Decision ladder: invalid if any relevant causal/accounting/input check fails; blocked if required contracts/support cannot qualify; rejected for predeclared failed benefit criteria within tested scope; inconclusive where uncertainty/support does not establish either conclusion; promising only if preregistered net economic, interval, support and stress criteria pass; independently supported only after separate qualified unused/prospective evaluation. Positive raw return, higher win rate or one better historical Sharpe alone is insufficient. Preserve all variants, retries, failures, exclusions, censoring and post-result exploratory amendments.

## 7. Missing capabilities and feasible builds

The [build register](BUILD_REGISTER.md) is part of acceptance: missing requirements cannot be deleted to make the design appear ready. Build from qualified ingredients: daily/calendar normalization, as-of joins, causal feature/pattern computations, manifests/adapters, paired accounting, label guard, trial registry, evaluator, receipts. Qualify/reuse existing project implementations before replacing them. Assign bounded implementation to SR-022/004/025/010/019 and family stories; if accepted scope cannot cover a shared component, create a separately bounded follow-up rather than silently expanding a released story.

Research source feasibility: earnings/consensus vintage, filings/fundamentals, insider routine history, event calendars, historical universe and quote persistence. A recorder may capture future first-seen/receipt clocks under authorized source access; it cannot invent retrospective clocks. SEC APIs offer submissions and extracted XBRL information; exact fact/accession/filing/units/restatement and dissemination clocks still require qualification. [SEC API documentation](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) read October 8, 2026 supports this acquisition-feasibility path, not proof of complete Form4, earnings-wire or historical first-seen coverage. Paid purchases require separate instructions; source-owned pipeline changes need its access/ownership rules.

Deferred/conditional needs are retained: order-level/venue/latency/auction evidence, borrow/short hedge accounting, option master/quotes/Greek/expiry models, non-equity curve/roll/funding and multi-asset long-horizon history. Their presence in the design does not activate M4, premium selling, ETF/futures/crypto/intraday research or M3 calls. If reconstruction is impossible and acquisition unavailable, record exact blocker and keep dependent research deferred.

## 8. Acceptance, handoff and privacy

Design acceptance requires exactly 76 unique source versions and 40 families with matching manifests/story/releases, all applicable requirement IDs and exact next qualification/blocker; four library variants stay distinct. [Templates](TEMPLATES.md) must cover input/source qualification, experiment preregistration, evaluation and release receipt. D1/D2/deferred walkthroughs must exercise independent arithmetic, future/prefix/clock/missingness/weight/scope cases and demonstrate no real historical access. Check against M2 negative trials, exposed periods, unknown arrival/fill authority and M4 exclusions.

Relevant CI, relationship checks, original-source hashes, privacy and exact delivered-source/artifact readback remain gates. Eight stages retained; Code review records agent evidence inspection/owner waiver, no independent reviewer claimed. Actual design publication is separate from any market-research release verdict. Freeze scoped acceptance before declaring SR-068 Done. No live action or unattended execution.

After verified design acceptance select one qualified M7 source/readiness question, provisionally D1 then D2 then E4; source feasibility may revise ordering with recorded rationale. Finish missing relevant contracts/builds before any separately registered historical experiment. Do not automatically launch M7, create a new chat, rerun M2, choose final dates or expand scope. All current design empirical budgets are zero historical comparisons and zero final evaluations.
