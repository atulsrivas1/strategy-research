# Required research templates v1

These templates are mandatory fields, not completed experiments. Every blank or unspecified required field blocks its dependent empirical admission. Mark an outcome-free field not applicable only with a scoped reason. Machine and human checks must use actual evidence rather than a checked box alone. [Shared design](DESIGN.md) and [build contracts](BUILD_REGISTER.md) apply.

## A. Intake and input qualification

- Record/story/source-version IDs, date/owner, exact question, mechanism, expected information value and falsifier; canonical rules/instrument/direction/horizon versus proposed adaptation and off-scope exclusions.
- Primary sources actually read, sections/access grade/version/date, external claims/counterevidence, inherited notes and local prior evidence; preserve inaccessible sources and differing variants.
- Every consumed field: security ID/symbol interval, source/provider/access/license owner, immutable dataset/artifact hash, schema/type/unit, formulas/dependencies/version, event/receipt/observed/publication/first-seen/available-at, revisions/adjustment/freshness/missingness.
- Decision/session/calendar/timezone/DST, eligible universe/action/delistings/warmup, overlap/duplicate source precedence, per-symbol/date coverage and admission authority; observed versus modeled availability must be distinct.
- Required R-contract IDs, existing evidence, missing need, feasibility class, exact bounded build/acquire/capture/defer path, owner/dependency and independently checked acceptance. Named gaps remain explicit.
- Qualification checks with independently expected values/categories/tolerances, actual evidence/commands/hashes, failures and precise qualified/restricted/blocked disposition. Source-reported PASS is not independent scientific acceptance.

## B. Preregistered experiment

- Immutable experiment/parent/story/scanner/context/evaluator/config IDs and pre-run time; all dependency/source/input/code/package/environment hashes and exact reproducible command.
- Frozen scanner formulas/parameters, eligibility/weights, signal/decision/entry/exit clocks and holding session definition; source adaptation boundary and reason for any retry.
- Context definitions for stock and market separately, one combined policy, aligned/conflicting/neutral/unknown actions/freshness, original opportunity weights/denominator, vetoed cash/no rescaling.
- Universe and point-in-time identity/actions; exact development/validation intervals/exposure labels; purge/embargo from real holding windows; final dates null/access disabled unless separately qualified. Guard before label I/O.
- Evidence level/order delay/fill/quote age/size/unfilled/censor/exit policy, fees/spread/slippage/impact/dividends/actions/turnover; capital/risk/overlap/integer/reservation conventions when funded.
- Finite trial/search budget INCLUDING every variant/parameter/overlay/control/model/selection attempt, registered remaining allowance and amendment policy; explicitly zero here until a later actual experiment is qualified.
- Numeric primary net incremental economic floor, minimum effective support, uncertainty precision/confidence/aggregation/resampling unit/block method/seed/repetitions, concentration limits, cost/execution stress and verdict criteria. Required numeric choices are unset in the design and cannot be filled after outcomes.
- Independent relevant small fixtures, package/adaptor/source parity, malformed-input/future-prefix tests, privacy/output allowlist and exact artifact manifest. Failed causality/accounting check invalidates dependent result.

## C. Evaluation and scoped lesson

- Frozen protocol versus actual executed variants, full failures/deviations/amendments; exact code/data/config and reproducibility receipt.
- Original opportunities, eligible/unknown/vetoed/unfilled/entered-censored/resolved counts; scanner/candidate exposure, turnover and cash; coverage cannot disappear from denominators.
- Paired net economics and declared uncertainty, avoided losses AND sacrificed winners, retained/vetoed costs, concentration/subperiod/regime/support and stress. State which price/fill/capital evidence level supports each metric.
- Invalid/blocked/rejected/inconclusive/promising verdict bounded to universe/horizon/period/cost assumptions; no winner-only total or confirmation from exposed history.
- Supported lesson, contrary evidence, uncertainty, conditions for revisiting, registry exposure/selection change, ranked next dependency-qualified question and exact process/resume status.

## D. Story/release delivery receipt

- Frozen scope, assigned stories/requirements/dependencies and accepted-versus-open gates; scientific verdict distinct from delivery completion.
- Sources actually read, full trial/failure/exclusion index, reports/commands/environment/manifests, independent relevant check evidence and applicable CI for final head.
- Actual author/committer attribution, agent evidence inspection and owner waiver; never label self-inspection independent approval. Eight stages retained.
- Sanitized publication allowlist excluding private source locations/data/ledgers/chats/credentials; exact published source/tree/artifact readback, tag/assets where release acceptance requires them.
- Exact unresolved blocker, scoped defer/rejection acceptance, lifecycle/milestone reconciliation, updated lessons/index/handoff and process status. No automatic next release/session/schedule.
