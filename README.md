# Strategy Research

Evidence-led research on short- and medium-term US individual-stock strategies, followed by bought-call studies when stock behavior is supported.

Current scope: long shares first; bought calls later; two to ten trading sessions; liquid large/mid-cap stocks. ETFs are deferred. Downward-move analysis is for risk and avoiding unsuitable long entries. No live trading or deployment is part of this project.

Work uses numbered stories, milestones, dependency-ready continuous pulling and one active implementation initially. Plans, correctness checks and evidence precede completion. Positive and unsuccessful experiments are retained. A completed research task may have an inconclusive or rejected finding.
